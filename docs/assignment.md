# Project assignment: Shigjetademy

Source: Shigjeta LLC, SHG-KS-PV-100-01

- Assigned developer: Elsa
- Final delivery deadline: 25 December 2026

## Project objective

Develop a SaaS education-management platform inspired by the centralized operational model of Deel, but created specifically for schools, kindergartens, academies, training centres and private lecturers.

Shigjetademy should allow education providers to manage their organizations, educators, student groups, payments, grades, attendance, learning materials, tests, performance and internal data from one platform.

## Required organization flow

1. Education provider creates an account.
2. Creates its school, kindergarten, academy or teaching organization.
3. Adds administrators and lecturers.
4. Creates courses and student groups.
5. Registers or invites students.
6. Assigns lecturers to groups.
7. Creates schedules and learning activities.
8. Manages attendance, grades, notes and performance.
9. Uploads and exchanges learning materials.
10. Creates tests and records results.
11. Manages student payments and financial status.

## Required user roles

- Platform administrator
- Organization owner
- School or academy administrator
- Lecturer or teacher
- Student
- Parent or guardian

- Locations and departments

### Lecturer management

- Lecturer profiles
- Assigned courses

- Lecturer performance information

### Student management

- Student profiles
- Student groups
- Enrolment information
- Attendance records
- Grades
- Behaviour and performance notes
- Uploaded work and documents

### Groups and courses

- Course creation
- Group creation
- Lecturer assignment
- Student enrolment
- Timetables
- Attendance
- Announcements
- Internal communication

### Learning materials

- Document and file uploads
- Materials organized by course or group
- Assignments
- Submission deadlines
- Student submissions
- Lecturer feedback

### Tests and performance

- Test creation
- Questions and answers
- Test completion
- Results
- Grade recording
- Individual performance overview
- Group-performance overview

### Payments

- Student fees
- Payment schedules
- Due dates
- Paid and unpaid status
- Payment records
- Receipts

### Dashboards

- Organization dashboard
- Lecturer dashboard
- Student dashboard
- Parent dashboard

The interface must be responsive and structured so users see only the information and actions connected to their role.

## Future development

The system architecture should support later additions such as:

- Automated online payments
- Lecturer payroll
- Video lessons
- Live classes
- AI-supported learning
- Advanced examinations
- Government or institutional reporting
- Mobile applications
- Education-provider marketplace

## Final delivery

By 25 December 2026, Elsa must deliver a deployed Shigjetademy platform with multi-organization support, role-based dashboards and working systems for users, groups, lecturers, students, attendance, grades, materials, tests, performance and payments.
