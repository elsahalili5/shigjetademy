# Homepage (marketing)

Scope: the Shigjetademy homepage, `client/src/App.tsx`. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: schools, academies, training centres, independent educators. Action: Book a demo. Proof: none exists; the product demonstrates itself with illustrative, labelled data. Constraints: no customer, metric, price or testimonial claims; use the supplied logo; clean, premium, professional, minimal (Deel as reference), fully responsive.

History: the Term Shelf (glass jar) direction was built and rejected by the user ("remove those glasses design"). The user took the category standard: a modern SaaS homepage in the Deel register, executed at full craft. Do not reintroduce jars, kraft labels or metaphor worlds.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product is the hero. A clean, confident SaaS homepage where the real Shigjetademy dashboard does the persuading; refuses decorative metaphors and stock illustration.

OWN-WORLD: White and cool off-white ground, logo navy as the committed field colour (hero stage, closing section), logo green as the single action and state colour. The logo's geometry (concentric arc segments cut by one diagonal) is the only ornament, drawn as crisp strokes on navy fields. Bricolage Grotesque display, Hanken Grotesk text.

STORY: The visitor reads what Shigjetademy is in one line, sees the dashboard running a real class, scans four capabilities, understands the six-step term workflow and who uses it, and books a demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT (revised 2026-10-08, full-bleed at user request): Nav with brand left, links centred in a pill, Log in + Book a demo right. Hero is a navy field; the AI-generated classroom photo (src/assets/photos/README.md) bleeds off the right and top edges, fading into navy on the left. White headline with "every class" in green and a drawn arc underline, lede, green Book a demo + outline See the platform, six module chips. Four floating product cards (illustrative data) and two icon badges sit along the photo's edges, clear of faces. "Built for" strip at the bottom of the navy. Phones: photo becomes a top band with two cards, copy follows on navy.

FORM: Category standard (canon exit), after seed key 063be7f6 rounds. Signature interaction: the arcs draw in and the notification cards stack in on load, Sonner-style; tabbed feature showcase with blur crossfade.
