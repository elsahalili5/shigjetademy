# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Schools, academies, training centres and independent educators who run teaching operations. The primary day-to-day user is deliberately undecided for now (user said it is not necessary yet); the marketing site addresses all four organization types.

## Product Purpose

Shigjetademy is an education-management platform (SaaS) that lets a teaching organization run its operations in one place. Success for the homepage: a qualified visitor understands what the platform does and books a demo.

## Positioning

One platform that covers the whole operational loop of a teaching organization, from enrolment through scheduling, attendance, payments, grading and communication. Sharper differentiation is an open decision.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities (at or near launch):
- Students and enrolment: student records, enrolment into courses/classes, groups.
- Scheduling and attendance: timetables, sessions, attendance tracking.
- Payments and invoicing: tuition fees, invoices, payment status.
- Grades, reports and messaging: assessments, progress reports, parent/student communication.

Stack: React 19 + TypeScript (Vite) in `client/`, Node/Express + TypeScript API in `server/`, npm workspaces.

Undecided: pricing, plans, integrations, languages/localization, mobile apps.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Shigjetademy (from Albanian "shigjeta", arrow).
- Logo supplied by the user: circular mark of a dark navy arc and diagonal arrow shaft with two green arc segments, wordmark "SHIGJETADEMY" curving around it. Colors in the logo: navy (~#22303F) and green (~#22A887). The logo must be used as supplied.
- The user asked for a modern, clean, premium, professional and minimal feel, with modern SaaS platforms like Deel as an explicit reference, but with its own identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo image (user supplied).
- No customers, testimonials, metrics, press, pricing, or certifications exist yet. Do not fabricate any of them. Product demonstration data in UI previews must be clearly illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. One place for the whole operation: every feature is shown as part of one connected loop, not a pile of modules.
2. Serves any size: from a single educator to a multi-branch academy.
3. Honest before impressive: show the product working instead of claiming results we do not have.
4. The primary action is booking a demo.
